What I Learned
- Prompt Engineering is the process of writing clear and specific instructions for AI.
- Better prompts help AI understand context, goals, and expectations.
- Vague prompts often lead to generic answers.
- Adding details, context, and constraints improves the quality of responses.
- Prompt Engineering saves time by reducing the need for follow-up questions.
- Well-structured prompts produce more accurate, actionable, and relevant outputs.
- The quality of AI responses depends heavily on the quality of the prompt provided.
